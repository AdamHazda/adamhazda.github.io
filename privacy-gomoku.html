1. Information Collection and Use

The App is designed to respect your privacy:

The App uses trusted third-party services that may collect certain technical data in accordance with their own respective privacy policies:

3. Security

The Developer ensures that the App remains secure and interacts exclusively with official Google services for ads and billing.
